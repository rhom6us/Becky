All lyrics from *Pop* by U2 (Island Records, 1997). All lyrics by Bono and The Edge. All music by U2.

The album's influence on the characters in this story is documented below, in order of appearance.

---

*"You know you're chewing bubble gum."*
"Discothèque" (p. __) — Found in Vivian Plume's handwriting, in a desk drawer. She'd been keeping a list.

*"Where is the hope, and where is the faith?"*
"If God Will Send His Angels" (p. __) — Francois, while persuading Tom to take the case. He doesn't notice.

*"Don't try too hard to think. Don't —"*
"Staring at the Sun" (p. __) — Francois, mid-persuasion. He loses the thread.

*"If there's an order in all of this disorder — I just need to know what it is."*
"Wake Up Dead Man" (p. __) — Francois, picking up where he left off. Still doesn't notice.

*"No emotional good night."*
"Gone" (p. __) — Francois, elaborating on the murder. His words, as far as he knows.

*"She felt the ground was giving way. Better off that way."*
"Last Night on Earth" (p. __) — Francois, asked why. His answer comes from somewhere else.

*"I'm not coming down, I'm not coming down!"*
"Gone" (p. __) — Francois, during the scissors fight. Screamed.

*"I'm already gone. Felt that way all along."*
"Gone" (p. __) — Francois, after the fight. The last thing he says before the light goes out.

*"There's an insect in your ear. If you scratch it, it won't disappear."*
"Staring at the Sun" (p. __) — Francois, through the observation window. Smiling.

*"You're not the only one staring at the sun — afraid of what you'd find if you stepped back inside."*
"Staring at the Sun" (p. __) — Tom, walking down the corridor. He thinks the words are his.
