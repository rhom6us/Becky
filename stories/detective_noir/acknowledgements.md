## Acknowledgements

This book exists because of Becky, who picked up a stuffed animal in the summer of 1997 and said — or maybe I said, or maybe nobody said — *we should write a story about this.* She then left for college and waited twenty-eight years for delivery, which is either extraordinary patience or the reasonable assumption that I'd never actually do it. Both interpretations are valid.

This book is *readable* because of my mother, who was asked to "just take a look" and came back with a full editorial intervention. She insists I asked for an edit. I insist I asked for a look. We have agreed to disagree, which in our family means she is right and I have agreed to stop bringing it up.

To be clear: she made it better. Substantially better. Every sentence she flagged deserved to be flagged, and every suggestion she made was the kind of suggestion you pretend to consider for a few days before quietly implementing word for word. She also, at a certain point, told me it was time to stop messing with it — which turned out to be the single most important editorial note in the entire process.

I did not stop messing with it.

She was right about that, too.

Thanks, Mom.
