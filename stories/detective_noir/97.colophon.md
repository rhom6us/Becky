## Colophon

This book was written in Microsoft Word, laid out in Adobe InDesign, retouched in Adobe Photoshop, and — in its final form — edited as a Markdown file in a Notes app by an artificial intelligence that, to its credit, never once asked why the zebra was on the boy's head.

The body text is set in Garamond — a typeface designed in the sixteenth century by a man who could not have anticipated this use case. The cover title is set in True Crimes, which is exactly what it sounds like. The back cover is set in EB Garamond, which is Garamond but open source, because even the fonts in this book have a duality problem.

The photographs were taken in 1997 on a disposable camera by Rebecca Stewart, who was leaving for college and had no idea she was creating evidence. They were developed at a drugstore. The total production budget for the visual component of this book was approximately $6.99.

Page layout and typesetting were performed by Pandoc and Typst, two pieces of software that did not exist when the photographs were taken, when the promise was made, or during most of the period in which the promise was not being kept.

The cover was designed in Photoshop by the author, who is not a graphic designer and would like that noted for the record.

No part of this book was produced on schedule.
