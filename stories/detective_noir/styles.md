# Forty Seconds — Style Guide

## Physical Specs

- **Trim size:** 5.5 × 8.5 inches (letter folded in half)
- **Binding:** Saddle stitch two-up, folded, glued
- **Content paper:** 24lb bond (~0.006" per sheet)
- **Cover:** Photo paper, folded and wrapped around content block, trimmed

## Page Count Estimate

| Section | Pages |
|---------|-------|
| Front matter (praise through epigraph) | 10 |
| Story (text + 8 images + section breaks) | 25 |
| Back matter (acknowledgements through colophon) | ~6 |
| **Total** | **~41** |

Round to nearest multiple of 4 for sheet nesting: **44 pages** = 22 sheets.

## Spine Thickness Estimate

- 22 sheets × 0.006" = 0.132"
- Glue layer: ~0.02"
- **Estimated spine: ~5/32" (4mm)**

Content pages will be trimmed ~1/16" on the spine edge to match the cover wrap. The gutter margin accounts for this.

---

## Margins (all pages unless noted)

Word mirror margins enabled. Inside/gutter are separate values.

| Edge | Measurement | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Inside** | 0.5" | Spine side |
| **Gutter** | 0.25" | Additional binding allowance (Word adds this to inside = 0.75" total) |
| **Outside** | 0.625" | Accounts for ~1/8" trim on the open edge; ~0.5" remains after trim |
| **Top** | 0.5" | Below page number |
| **Bottom** | 0.625" | Slightly deeper for visual balance |

The outside edge (opposite the spine) is trimmed to match the cover wrap. Trim amount ≈ spine thickness (~1/8"). The outside margin is set to 0.625" so that ~0.5" remains after trimming.

Page numbers sit at the top of the page, outside edge, within the top margin area.

## Images

- **Width:** 100% of text block (margin to margin)
- **Captions:** None — suppressed throughout
- **Spacing:** 8pt above, 8pt below
- **Alignment:** Centered within text block

---

## Page-by-Page Formatting

### 01 — Advance Praise (recto, no page number)
- Header: Section Header style
- Blurbs: Blurb Quote style; attributions: Blurb Attribution style
- Can overflow to verso (page 2)

### 03 — Half Title (recto, no page number)
- "Forty Seconds": Half Title style
- Positioned in upper third of page; nothing else on this page

### 04 — Also By (verso, no page number)
- Header: Section Header style
- Category headers: Also By Category style; titles: Also By Title style; annotations: Also By Annotation style

### 05 — Title Page (recto, no page number)
- "Forty Seconds": Half Title style
- "Thomas Butler": Title Author style
- "Photographs by Rebecca Stewart": Title Photo Credit style
- "Disposable Camera Press": Publisher style

### 06 — Copyright (verso, no page number)
- All text: Copyright style

### 07 — Dedication (recto, no page number)
- *For Becky, obviously.*: Dedication style
- Centered horizontally, positioned in upper third

### 08 — Blank (verso)

### 09 — Epigraph (recto, no page number)
- Quote: Epigraph Quote style
- "— U2, 'Mofo'": Epigraph Attribution style
- Upper third of page

### 10 — Blank (verso)

### 11+ — Story (recto start, page numbers begin)

- Chapter numbers: Chapter Number style. No page number on chapter-opening pages.
- Body text: Normal style; italics: Emphasis style.
- Images: full-width, no captions, 8pt spacing above and below.
- Section breaks (`---`): blank line, no horizontal rule.
- Page numbers begin here: Page Number style, top outside edge.
